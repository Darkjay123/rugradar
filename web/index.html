Questions people ask

Do I need to connect my wallet?

No. RugRadar only reads public data. There is nothing to sign and we never ask for a recovery phrase. Anyone who does is scamming you.

Does "low risk" mean it's safe?

No. It means we found no major red flags right now. Tokens can change fast, so only put in money you can afford to lose.

What does the test buy and sell do?

We simulate buying and selling the token without spending anything. If the sell fails or the tax is huge, that's a honeypot: you can buy but never get out.

Can I use it in Telegram or my AI tool?

Yes. There's a Telegram bot that can also warn you if a token turns bad, and RugRadar is listed on the official MCP Registry so AI assistants can run checks too.
